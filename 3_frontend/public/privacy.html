Sekaa Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Sekaa ("we", "us") operates the Sekaa meme-sharing app and sekaa.me website. This page explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

Account info (name, handle, email, avatar), content you post (memes, captions, comments, statuses), and usage data (likes, saves, follows, chat messages between users).

How We Use It

To operate core app features: showing your feed, enabling follows/likes/comments, and delivering chat messages between users you message directly.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Content you post publicly (memes, comments, profile info) is visible to other users by design.

Data Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from Settings inside the app. This removes your profile and account access. Some previously-posted content may take additional time to be fully removed from our systems.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@sekaa.me
